Fit any AI image to the exact shape and size your A+ Content slot needs — nothing stretched, nothing uploaded.

A+ Content is the extra pictures-and-text section on your book's Amazon page. Pick the same module you will choose in KDP's A+ Content Manager, so every picture gets the right shape.

2

Add your image

PNG or JPG. Drag it in, click to browse, or paste with Ctrl+V.

3

Choose how it fits

Your picture is probably a different shape than the slot. "Fill the frame" crops the edges so the picture covers the whole slot — KDP crops to the slot shape anyway, so this is usually the best choice. "Fit inside" keeps the whole picture and fills the leftover space.

Zooming in crops closer and uses fewer of your picture's pixels, so the picture gets softer.

4

Choose size and file type

KDP only gives you a minimum size, and it lets you zoom into your picture. "Sharp" makes the file twice that size so it stays crisp. We never stretch your picture past what it really has — if it can't fill that size, we use the biggest size it can. KDP accepts JPG or PNG files up to 3 MB.
